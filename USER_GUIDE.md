# marathakalyanam — user guide

Welcome to Maratha Kalyanam, a matrimonial site for Telugu-Maratha people in Andhra Pradesh
and Telangana.

## What is Maratha Kalyanam?

Maratha Kalyanam is a matchmaking platform built for the Telugu-Maratha community. We help you
find a life partner by connecting profiles of brides and grooms within the community. All
profiles are reviewed by our team before being published, ensuring quality and safety.

## Creating an account

1. Visit [marathakalyanam.com](https://marathakalyanam.com) and click **Register**
2. Enter your email address and choose a password (at least 8 characters)
3. We'll send you a verification link to your email
4. Click the link to verify your email
5. You're ready to create your first profile

## Adding a profile

You can add profiles for yourself or other family members (e.g. siblings, children).

1. Log in and go to **My Profiles**
2. Click **New Profile**
3. Fill in all required fields:
   - Personal: name, date of birth, height, complexion, education, occupation, annual income
   - Location: city, state, country
   - Astrology: gotra, kuldevata, devak, surname/clan, nakshatram, rashi
   - Diet: vegetarian, non-vegetarian, or eggetarian
   - Manglik: whether affected by Mangal dosha (yes/no/partial/unknown)
   - About you: describe yourself and what you're looking for in a partner
4. Click **Save as Draft**

Your profile is now saved as a draft (visible only to you). You can edit it anytime.

When ready, click **Submit for Approval**. Our team will review it within a few days and either
approve it (so it appears in searches) or ask for changes.

## Profile fields explained

- **Gender:** Bride or groom
- **Date of birth:** Used to calculate age and astrological details
- **Height (cm):** Your height in centimeters (e.g. 160 for 5'3")
- **Complexion:** Fair, medium, dark, etc.
- **Education:** Degree or field of study (e.g. "B.Tech Computer Science")
- **Occupation:** Your profession (e.g. "Software Engineer", "Doctor", "Teacher")
- **Annual income (optional):** Gross annual income in INR; private and not shown in search results
- **Gotra:** Your patrilineal clan (e.g. "Gautam", "Bhrigu")
- **Kuldevata:** Family deity (e.g. "Hanuman", "Durga")
- **Devak:** Astrological classification (helps with birth-chart compatibility)
- **Nakshatram:** Your birth star (used for horoscope matching)
- **Rashi:** Your astrological sun sign (zodiac)
- **Manglik:** Does your horoscope show Mangal dosha? (important for many families)
- **Mother tongue:** Language spoken at home (default: Telugu)
- **Diet:** Vegetarian, non-vegetarian, or eggetarian
- **About you:** A personal note about yourself (interests, values, what you're seeking)
- **Partner expectations:** What you're looking for in a match

**Privacy:** Only approved profiles appear in search results. Searchers see:
- Your first initial, age, height, education, occupation, city, gotra, nakshatram, rashi, diet
- A blurred photo (not your face clearly visible)
- NOT your full name, income, contact info, or clear photos

You can edit any field, but if your profile was already approved, re-submitting it will send
it back for re-review.

## Uploading photos

Maratha Kalyanam accepts **passport-style photos only** — single face, neutral background, face
clearly visible. No group photos, graphics, or alternative styles.

1. Go to your profile and click **Add Photo**
2. Choose a JPEG or PNG file (max 10 MB)
3. The system will:
   - Check that it contains exactly one face (using AI face detection)
   - Auto-crop to frame the face
   - Resize to standard portrait dimensions (413 × 531 pixels)
   - Compress to ~500 KB for fast loading

4. If approved, the photo will appear as:
   - **Clear photo:** visible only to you (for personal reference)
   - **Blurred photo:** shown in search results (people see a blurred version)
   - **Thumbnail:** used in admin interface

If a photo is rejected (e.g. no face detected, multiple faces, or poor quality), you'll see a
message and can retake it immediately.

You can upload up to **5 photos** per profile. The first one will be your primary photo (shown
in search).

## Searching for matches

1. Go to **Search** (no login required)
2. Optionally filter by:
   - Gender (bride or groom)
   - Age range
   - Gotra, nakshatram, rashi, diet, manglik status
   - Location (city, state)
3. Results show approved profiles with partial information and blurred photos
4. Click a profile to see more details (still blurred photos)

## Requesting full details

When you find a profile you're interested in:

1. Click **Request Full Details**
2. Optionally leave a message (e.g. "Hi, I'd like to know more")
3. Our team reviews your request (usually within 24–48 hours)
4. If approved, you'll receive an email with:
   - Full profile information (including income, contact details if shared)
   - Clear, full-size photos
   - The requester's contact info so you can connect directly

**Note:** requesting details shows the profile owner that you're interested. Be respectful and
sincere in your request message.

## Editing and deleting profiles

**Edit:** Click **Edit** on any of your profiles. Change any field and click **Save**. If your
profile was approved, re-editing and saving will re-submit it for re-approval.

**Delete:** Click **Delete** to permanently remove a profile. This cannot be undone.

## FAQ

**Why are photos blurred in search results?**
To respect privacy. Only if someone requests your full details (and admin approves) will they
see clear photos.

**Why does my profile need admin approval?**
To ensure quality, safety, and that all profiles are genuine members of the community.

**How long does approval take?**
Usually 24–48 hours. Our team reviews during business hours.

**Can I have multiple profiles?**
Yes, you can add profiles for yourself and family members (e.g. siblings). Each profile is
reviewed independently.

**How do I delete my account?**
Currently you can delete individual profiles, but to fully delete your account and all data,
please email **ramanamborespam@gmail.com** with your request. We'll remove all traces of your
account within 5 business days.

**Is my information safe?**
Yes. We use encrypted connections, secure passwords, and don't share your details with anyone
without your consent. Only approved detail requests reveal full information.

**What if I see inappropriate behavior?**
Please report it immediately to **ramanamborespam@gmail.com**. We take community safety
seriously.

**Can I contact someone without requesting full details?**
Not yet. Our system ensures mutual interest: both parties must approve a request before
exchanging contact info. This prevents spam and protects privacy.

**I'm having technical issues. What should I do?**
Email **ramanamborespam@gmail.com** with a description of the issue, and we'll help.
